# Publishable captures

These captures may be versioned because they contain no private identifiers.
Original Ethernet captures remain under `data/local/evidence/` until processed
by `src/lab/sanitize_capture.py`. Sanitized Ethernet NPZ files are listed in
[`ethernet/README.md`](ethernet/README.md).

PoE oscilloscope captures and regeneration commands are listed in
[`poe/README.md`](poe/README.md).
